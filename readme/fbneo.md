## Romsets

Use romsets of FinalBurn Neo 1.0.0.3. No ROMs or BIOS are included. It was tested with CPS2 games (Dungeons & Dragons: Tower of Doom and Shadow over Mystara), which ran at full speed.

## Performance

Heavy boards can run below full speed: Mortal Kombat (Midway T-Unit) ran at about 39 FPS of 54.7.

`Cyclone` (`fbneo-cyclone`) is off. With it on, a CPS2 game used about 12% less CPU; the sound and the savestates were not checked with it on.
